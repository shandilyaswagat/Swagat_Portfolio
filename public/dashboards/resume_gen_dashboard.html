About

Resume Generator is a tool I built to make one part of job searching a little less tedious: tailoring your resume for each role.

Why I built it

A good resume isn't necessarily the same resume for every job.

But tailoring it usually means going back to the job description, figuring out what matters, rewriting a few bullet points, and doing the formatting all over again.

I wanted a simpler way to do that while keeping my actual experience at the center.

How it works

01 · Start with your resume
Your existing resume stays the foundation.
02 · Add the job description
Paste the JD for the role you're applying to. The company and role can be picked up automatically.
03 · Tailor it
The relevant skills, keywords, and experience are brought forward based on the role.
04 · Export
Download the finished resume as a PDF or Word document.

Keep it real

The point isn't to make you look qualified for something you've never done.

Your experience stays your experience. Resume Generator is there to help you present the right parts of it for the role you're applying for.
