About Me

The person behind Job Pipeline.

I'm Swagat, a data analyst and builder who enjoys turning messy, repetitive processes into systems that are easier to understand and use.

BACKGROUND

I started my career working in the insurance domain, where I worked with Guidewire PolicyCenter and became increasingly interested in the data and process side of the work. Over time, my focus expanded toward data analysis, visualization, automation, and building practical tools that solve real problems.

I work with SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel, and automation tools, but I'm more interested in how they come together to solve a problem than in the tools themselves.

WHY I BUILT JOBHUNT

Job searching is a process made up of many repetitive tasks.

finding relevant roles, understanding job requirements, tailoring a resume, applying, reaching out to recruiters, and keeping track of everything.

I built JobHunt to bring those pieces into one system and automate as much of the repetitive work as possible.

It started as a personal solution to a problem I was dealing with myself. Building it also gave me a way to combine my experience in data, automation, and software development into something I could actually use.

Find   →   Tailor   →   Apply   →   Outreach   →   Track
HOW I BUILD

I prefer simple systems over complicated ones.

When a process feels unnecessarily difficult, I usually start by breaking it down, understanding where the friction comes from, and then finding a way to make the process clearer and more repeatable.

That approach is reflected in the way I build JobHunt and the other projects I work on.

CURRENTLY

I'm continuing to improve JobHunt while exploring new ways to use data, automation, and AI to build practical products.

For me, the goal isn't to add technology for the sake of it. It's to build something that genuinely makes a complicated task easier.
